Seeking Overweight/Obese Males & Females

Overweight/ Obese Male & Female Volunteers (SAPPHIRE SAD 2)

Pharmaron located in downtown Baltimore, is currently seeking overweight/obese male & female volunteers to participate in a clinical research study to evaluate a study drug which is being developed as a possible treatment for weight reduction.

To qualify you must be:

  • Overweight/Obese Male or Female
  • Ages: 18-65 at screening
  • BMI ≥ 27 at screening
  • Non-smoker
  • This study will include a 5 Day inpatient stay with 19 outpatient visits

Qualified participants will be compensated up to $8,100 for their study-related time and travel. 

To register for a clinical trial, contact our recruitment department at 240.673.0900 or send an email to baltimore.recruiter@pharmaron-us.com.

Seeking Healthy Adult Males & Females

Healthy Male & Female Volunteers (CHESAPEAKE)

Pharmaron located in downtown Baltimore, is currently seeking healthy male & female volunteers to participate in a clinical research study to evaluate a study drug which is being developed as a possible treatment for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD).

To qualify you must be:

  • Healthy Male or Female
  • Ages: 18-65 at screening
  • Non-smoker
  • This study will include an inpatient period of 18 days/17 nights with a phone call on Day 28

Qualified participants will be compensated up to $8,600 for their study-related time and travel. 

To register for a clinical trial, contact our recruitment department at 240.673.0900 or send an email to baltimore.recruiter@pharmaron-us.com.
