# Read some Clinics’ own sites, and never the ones that say no

ClinicalTrials.gov does not say what a study pays or how long the stay is. Clinics say it on their own recruitment sites. GuineaPing reads the study lists of the Clinics whose sites do not forbid it, once an hour, and shows each Opening in the Clinic’s own words with a link to its page. For every other Clinic it shows only a link, where it has one, and the Member reads the page themselves. A Clinic with no list GuineaPing can link to is still shown, so a Member can watch for its Trials (since 2026-11-24).

The Clinics that are read have not been asked. Their sites say nothing about automated reading, and the owner decided on 2026-11-22 to assume permission, because GuineaPing is a private group, is not a paid app, and does not need the Openings to be built.

On 2026-11-23 the owner added Tips: a Member may send the owner money through a payment service of their own, and GuineaPing shows where. The owner’s view is that this leaves GuineaPing unpaid, because nothing is shown, hidden or sent sooner according to whether a Member tips, and GuineaPing does not know who has. It is still money reaching the owner from a group that sees Openings read from Clinics’ sites, so it weakens “not a paid app” as a reason, most of all for Pfizer, whose terms allow noncommercial personal reference only. The owner chose to keep reading every source, Pfizer included. The research behind the choice of Clinics is in `docs/research/paid-study-sources.md` (pull request #19).

## Rules the Clinic read keeps

- **Only listed sources.** A Clinic is read only if it has a reader in `src/worker/openings/sources.ts`. Clinics whose sites block scripts are link-only. So is every Clinic GuineaPing has no address for, which shows with no link at all.
- **Neither robots.txt nor the terms of use decide (since 2026-11-24).** A Clinic may be read whatever its robots.txt or its website terms say. Earlier that day the owner chose robots.txt over the terms, in his words: “We respect robots.txt files but we also understand this is an app used by humans so any scraping is the exact same activity users are already taking more often than this app will by checking constantly throughout the day, we are reducing the load on the server.” Later the same day he set robots.txt aside too: “there are no legal requirements to respecting robots.txt and as this is a product for improving humanity were going to ignore robots.txt restrictions at the moment.” Seven Clinics’ terms forbid bots in so many words: ICON, CenExel, Trialmed, Medpace, Parexel, Syneos and Dr. Vince (quoted in `docs/research/paid-study-sources.md`). Every Clinic’s robots.txt was read that day: all allow their study list except K2’s, which forbids everything; abbviephase1.com has none. What still rules a Clinic out is its site turning GuineaPing away (see “A no is final”): GuineaPing does not get round a refusal or a challenge.
- **Once an hour.** Each source is read at most once an hour, one source per run, the runs three minutes apart (six minutes apart until 2026-11-24, when there came to be more sources than runs). It was once a day until 2026-11-23. The owner changed it so that a Member can hear of a new Opening within the hour: paid tip channels pass studies on within minutes, and a day-old list is no use against them. The research this decision rests on (`docs/research/paid-study-sources.md`) named reading at most once a day as one of the conditions that keep assuming permission reasonable; the owner chose to go past that condition.
- **A no is final.** If a site answers 401, 403 or 429, or shows a challenge page, reading that source stops until a person clears it.
- **No disguise.** Requests carry no browser identity. The owner chose not to send an identifying User-Agent either, so requests set no headers at all. Since 2026-11-24, in wording the owner approved: “Where a Clinic’s own list page sends its data call as a form, as Parexel’s does, GuineaPing sends the same form, with no sign-in and no cookies. The form goes as the request’s body, and the runtime adds the one header that says so.”
- **A list page’s own data call (since 2026-11-24).** Some list pages fetch their studies as data. That data call may be read instead of the page, with the same plain request, or with the page’s own form where it sends one (see “No disguise”). AbbVie’s is read this way. Its page sends a sign-in token with the call. The owner opened the call in a browser with no token on 2026-11-24, and it answered. A call that needs a token, a key or a captcha is not read: GuineaPing does not sign in or get round a check. A call that starts refusing is a no (see “A no is final”). The call may be on another company’s host, as Clinical Pharmacology of Miami’s is on its recruitment company’s. Its Openings still link only to the Clinic’s own site.
- **The Clinic’s words.** Text is stored and shown as plain text, unchanged. What a Member reads is never worked out from it. Dates and amounts are not otherwise interpreted, but three things are read out of the words and never shown, each only to order or limit the Openings a Member sees: the highest pay figure, to order by pay (since 2026-11-23); and the number of Follow-up visits and the day an Opening starts, so that a Member can limit the list by either (both since 2026-11-24). Links are kept only if they are https on the Clinic’s own host. AbbVie’s feed gives some facts as bare values. Its pay “4200.00” is shown as “$4,200”, and its age “18-45” and BMI “18-32” as “Age 18-45” and “BMI 18-32”. The values are unchanged.
- **Inside the group.** Openings are shown only to Members.
- **Pfizer.** Its terms limit copying to personal reference, so only the title, pay, who it is for, and the link are kept.

## Considered Options

- **Ask each Clinic first**: the careful route. Set aside for now by the owner; still the way to add a Clinic whose terms forbid reading.
- **Screenshots of the pages**: still an automated tool visiting the site, so it changes nothing for Clinics that forbid that, and it is heavier and less reliable than text for the ones that do not.
- **A Clinic Watch on ClinicalTrials.gov**: alerts when a new Trial names a Clinic. Needs no permission. Not built yet; it would sit beside this, not replace it.

## Consequences

- robots.txt for each source was checked by hand on 2026-11-22 and allowed the list. The read does not fetch robots.txt, so it must be checked again by hand when a source is added or a Clinic changes its site.
- Each source has its own parser. A redesign makes the parser find nothing; the read then keeps the Openings it had, reports the problem once, and tries again the next hour.
- Reading every hour makes it more likely that a site answers 429 one day. That is still a no: the source goes quiet until a person clears it.
- To clear a refusal, a person checks the Clinic’s site and terms, then sets `refused_at` to NULL for that source in `clinic_sources`.
- If a Clinic asks GuineaPing to stop, remove its reader from `sources.ts`. Its Clinic entry stays, as a link.
- Openings have no Alerts. A Member sees them on the Clinics page, and can ask, Clinic by Clinic, for a Push when a read finds a new one there (since 2026-11-23). That Push names the Clinic and gives the entry’s title, pay, dates and stay, which a glance at the phone would show; the Member is told so where they choose.
