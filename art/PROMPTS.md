# Grok prompts — round 2

Paste **Style**, then the **Cast** line for whoever is in the scene, then the scene, then **Finish**.

**Style**
> Flat vector illustration, simple geometric shapes, thick rounded outlines, limited palette of mint green, soft coral, cream and charcoal, no gradients, plain off-white background, friendly mobile app mascot style.

**Cast** (matches round 1, so the set stays consistent)
- **Pip** (main mascot): a chubby tan-and-cream guinea pig with a cream stripe down the forehead, floppy light-brown ears, a small coral nose, round black eyes and dark grey feet.
- **Dr. Clover**: a coral-and-cream guinea pig in mint-green scrubs with a stethoscope and a small ID badge.
- **Break-room crew**: a white guinea pig with pink ears in lavender scrubs, a ginger one in navy scrubs, a black-and-white one in pink scrubs, and a tan one in teal scrubs.

**Finish**
> Single centered character, full body visible, generous empty space around it, square 1:1, no text, no words, no letters, no logos.

## Sets that save time

**Pose sheet (stickers):**
> Character sheet of Pip in six poses arranged in a 3×2 grid with even spacing: waving, sleeping curled up, surprised with paws on cheeks, thinking with paw on chin, cheering with arms up, pointing to the right. Same proportions and colors in every pose. No text.

**App icon:**
> App icon: just Pip's head and shoulders, front-facing, big friendly eyes, a small golden bell tucked behind one ear, centered on a solid mint green rounded square, very bold outlines, minimal detail so it reads at 48 pixels. No text.

**Spot icons (to swap the plain empty-state text):**
> Set of 6 small spot icons in a 3×2 grid, each a simple object with thick rounded outlines: a mint bell, a coral heart, a mint clipboard with check, a charcoal pair of binoculars, a coral map pin, a mint magnifying glass. No text.

## Tips

- Keep each prompt to one character and one action. Grok gets muddled with three or more props.
- If the colors drift, add: "use only these colors: mint #9FD1BF, coral #F28B7A, tan #E0AE7A, cream #FDF8EE, charcoal #2E2E33".
- To keep Pip on-model, upload `art/source/alert-bell.png` as a reference image and add "same character and style as the reference".
- Grok segments props into separate layers; ask for props to sit **beside** the character, not overlapping, so the cutouts come out without holes.
